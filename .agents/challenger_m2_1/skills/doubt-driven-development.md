## Core Review Scope
- Fresh-context adversarial posture: Biased to disprove, not approve.
- Scrutinize implicit and unverified assumptions.
- Look for edge cases not handled, failure modes under unexpected input, hidden coupling.
- Rigorous disproof attempts via empirical test execution.
